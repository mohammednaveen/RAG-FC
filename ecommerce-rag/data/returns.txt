DOCUMENT: RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
DOCUMENT_ID: RETURN-2026
LAST_UPDATED: September 2026

==================================================
SECTION 1 — RETURN ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Customers can request a return within 15 days of delivery.

The return period begins on the date the product is
delivered to the customer.

==================================================
SECTION 2 — PRODUCT CONDITION
==================================================

Products must be unused and returned with their original
packaging, accessories, manuals, and other items supplied
with the product.

Products showing damage caused by customer misuse may not
be eligible for a refund.

==================================================
SECTION 3 — REFUND PROCESS
==================================================

Refunds are processed after the returned product passes
inspection.

Approved refunds are normally credited within 5–7 business
days after the return is approved.

The exact time required for the refund to appear may vary
depending on the customer's payment provider.

==================================================
SECTION 4 — RETURN SHIPPING
==================================================

Shipping charges for eligible returns are covered by the
company.

Customers may be responsible for return shipping costs
when the return is caused by reasons outside the company's
return policy.

==================================================
SECTION 5 — WARRANTY CLAIMS
==================================================

Warranty claims are handled separately according to the
warranty terms of the individual product.

A warranty claim is not automatically treated as a normal
product return.

==================================================
SECTION 6 — EXCHANGES
==================================================

Customers may request an exchange instead of a refund
when the requested product is eligible and replacement
stock is available.

If replacement stock is unavailable, the customer may
receive a refund after the returned product passes
inspection.
