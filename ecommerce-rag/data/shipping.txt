DOCUMENT: SHIPPING POLICY
DOCUMENT_ID: SHIP-2026
LAST_UPDATED: September 2026

==================================================
SECTION 1 — STANDARD DELIVERY
==================================================

Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 business days.

Delivery time is calculated from the date the order is
dispatched, not from the date the order is placed.

==================================================
SECTION 2 — EXPRESS DELIVERY
==================================================

Express delivery usually takes 1–2 business days.

Express delivery costs ₹199 per order.

Express delivery is available only for eligible
PIN codes.

==================================================
SECTION 3 — FREE SHIPPING
==================================================

Orders above ₹2,000 qualify for free standard shipping.

Express delivery charges are not waived by the free
shipping policy.

==================================================
SECTION 4 — ORDER PROCESSING
==================================================

Orders are normally dispatched within 24 hours after
payment confirmation.

Orders placed during major sales may require additional
processing time.

==================================================
SECTION 5 — DELIVERY DELAYS
==================================================

Delivery times may be longer during major sales, public
holidays, severe weather, or unexpected courier delays.

The estimated delivery date shown during checkout is
not a guaranteed delivery date.

==================================================
SECTION 6 — ORDER TRACKING
==================================================

Customers receive a tracking link after the order has
been dispatched.

The tracking link can be used to view the latest courier
status of the shipment.
